Eco-Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Eco-Bot may receive information provided through Instagram's messaging platform, including an Instagram user ID, username, and messages sent to the bot.

2. How We Use Information

The information received by Eco-Bot is used to provide its features and functionality, including commands, virtual currency, profiles, inventory, experience, and gameplay.

Eco-Bot may store gameplay-related information such as virtual currency, experience points, inventory items, cooldowns, levels, jobs, and other game statistics.
